Hot by Science is a health platform that connects you with a medical team, personalized care plans, and lab testing. These docs explain how to use each part of the platform.

## Product areas

Hot by Science has three product areas. Each one has its own sign-in and is built for a different audience.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Care" icon="heart-pulse" href="/care/your-account">
    For patients. Create an account, complete a short health assessment, and receive a care plan reviewed by our doctors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CMS" icon="user-doctor">
    For the Hot by Science team. Manage patients and review clinical lab results.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Labs" icon="flask">
    For anyone. Browse lab test products and check out online.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get started with Care

If you're a new patient, start by creating your Care account. You need an email address you can access and a phone number.

  Create an account, verify your email, sign in, and reset your password.
</Card>
