The **Clinical & Lab** page is where you manage each patient's lab work. Use it to create lab requisitions, track patients through each lab stage, and mark requisitions as sent to the lab.

## Prerequisites

- You are [signed in to the CMS](/cms/sign-in) as an admin.

## Open the Clinical & Lab page

The CMS opens **Clinical & Lab** after you sign in. You can also click **Clinical & Lab** in the sidebar.

The top of the page shows how many patients are at each stage:

- **Lab Requisition Sending**: Care plans waiting for a requisition to be sent.
- **Lab Results Inputting**: Requisitions sent to the lab and waiting for results.
- **Care Plan Reviewing**: Care plans in clinical review.
- **Active Care Plan**: Active care plans.

## Filter patients by lab status

Each tab below the counts lists patients at one stage. The page opens on the **Requisitions** tab.

| Tab | Patients shown |
| --- | --- |
| **All activity** | Every patient, regardless of care plan status. |
| **Requisitions** | Patients with a requisition pending. |
| **Lab Results** | Patients whose requisition was sent and who are waiting for lab results. |
| **Care Plans Review** | Patients whose care plan is in clinical review. |
| **Active Care Plans** | Patients with an active care plan. |

Each tab has a **Search by name, email or phone number** box and shows the patient's name, phone, email, updated date, and status. The selected tab is kept in the page URL, so going back from a patient returns you to the same tab.

Click a patient row to open that patient's detail page.

## Create a lab requisition

A lab requisition orders the tests that are due for a patient's care plan. The CMS picks the lab panel from the care plan, so you don't choose tests yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    On the **Requisitions** tab, click **Create Lab Requisition**. The **Create Requisition** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a patient">
    In **Patient**, search for and select the patient. Only patients with a care plan pending a requisition appear in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the required tests">
    The **Required tests** section shows the tests the requisition will order, along with any collection instructions. The tests are read-only.

    If the CMS can't create a requisition for the patient, it shows the reason instead of the tests and disables **Create Requisition**. For example, the patient already has an open requisition or no lab is due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    Optionally, add specific requirements, sample handling notes, or clinical background in **Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the requisition">
    Click **Create Requisition**. The patient appears on the **Requisitions** tab with the **Pending Requisition** status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mark a requisition as sent

After you send a requisition to the lab, mark it as sent so the patient moves to the next stage.

1. On the **Requisitions** tab, click the actions menu in the patient's row.
2. Click **Mark as Sent**.

The CMS marks the patient's pending requisition as sent. The patient's status changes to **Sent to Lab**, and the patient moves to the **Lab Results** tab.

The actions available in a row depend on the patient's status:

| Status | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending Requisition** | **Mark as Sent**, **Cancel**, **Download** |
| **Sent to Lab** | **Enter Results**, **Mark as Completed**, **Download** |
| **Processing** | **Update Results**, **Mark as Completed**, **Download** |

## Download requisition details

Download a patient's lab requisition details as a PDF to share with the lab or keep for your records.

1. Click the patient's row to open their detail page.
2. Click **Download**.

The CMS saves an A4 PDF of the patient header and care plan sections as they look on screen. The **Download** button shows a loading state while lab results load, so the PDF includes them. The file is named after the patient and the current date:

```text
lab-requisition-julian-webb-2026-10-12.pdf
```

If the PDF can't be generated, the CMS shows "The PDF could not be generated. Please try again."
