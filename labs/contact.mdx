Use the **Contact Us** page to reach the HBS Labs team with questions about HBS Labs, your order, shipping, or research products. Open it from the **Contact Us** link in the site footer, or go to `/contact`.

The **Contact Us** page is always reachable. The research-use entry gate doesn't appear on it.

<Warning>
  Don't use the contact form for medical emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or seek immediate medical attention.
</Warning>

## Send a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in **Name** and **Email Address**. The email address must be valid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a topic">
    Under **What can we help you with?**, select one of:

    - **General Question**
    - **Orders & Shipping**
    - **Other**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Enter a **Subject** and a **Message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send message**. After HBS Labs receives your message, the form clears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

All fields are required.

## Email support

You can also email the team directly at [support@hbslabs.com](mailto:support@hbslabs.com). The address appears under **Platform Support & Inquiries** on the **Contact Us** page and in the site footer.
