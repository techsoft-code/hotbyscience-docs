Products appear on the HBS Labs landing page, grouped into **Shape** and **Glow** sections. Each product has its own detail page with images, variants, and a full description.

## Product cards

Each product card on the landing page shows:

- The product thumbnail, name, and price.
- A short description, trimmed to two lines.
- An **Add to Cart** button.
- An **_N_ added** badge when the product is already in your cart. The count adds up every variant of that product in your cart.

Click anywhere on the card, other than **Add to Cart**, to open the product's detail page.

### Add to cart from a product card

What happens when you click **Add to Cart** depends on whether the product has variants:

- **No variants**: HBS Labs adds one unit of the product to your cart right away.
- **Has variants**: a variant picker opens. It's a bottom sheet on mobile and a dialog on larger screens.

In the variant picker, you can:

1. Select a variant under **Select Variant**. The first variant is selected by default.
2. Set the quantity with the **+** and **−** buttons.
3. Click **Add to Cart**.

To see everything about the product before adding it, click **View product details** in the picker.

## Product detail page

The detail page is at `/products/{product-name}`, where the last part is a readable version of the product name. For example, `/products/ghk-cu`. When you share a product link, link previews and search results show that product's own title, description, and preview image.

Breadcrumbs at the top link back to **Home** and **Product list**. The page shows:

- **Image gallery**: product images. On larger screens, the gallery stays in view while you scroll the details.
- **Name and tags**: the product name and any product tags.
- **Price**: the price of the selected variant.
- **Select Level**: the available variants.
- **Short description**: a one-line summary, shown in bold above the full description.
- **Description**: the full product description.
- **Important research disclaimer**: product-specific research-use notes, when available.

### Variants and levels

Variants are shown as numbered levels, ordered from lowest to highest, with the amount of each. For example:

```text
Level 1: 100mg   Level 2: 200mg   Level 3: 300mg
```

Selecting a level updates the price. If a product has only one variant, HBS Labs shows its amount as preselected, and there's nothing to choose.

### Add to cart from the detail page

1. Select a level.
2. Set the quantity with the quantity stepper. The minimum is 1.
3. Click **Add to Cart**.

On mobile, the quantity stepper and **Add to Cart** button stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.

Adding the same product and variant from a product card and from its detail page puts it on the same cart line. HBS Labs increases the quantity instead of adding a duplicate line.

If HBS Labs can't load a product, the page shows a **Connection Issue** warning and **Product Not Found**. Try again, or return to the landing page.

## Added-to-cart confirmation

After you add an item, a notification confirms it, for example **2 items added to Cart**. The notification shows:

- The product image, name, and price.
- A summary of the category and variant, such as `SHAPE, Level 2: 200mg`.
- A **View Cart** button that opens your cart.

If you add several items in a row, each new notification replaces the previous one.
