Your cart holds the products you plan to order. Because HBS Labs is guest-only, the cart is saved in your browser rather than in an account. It stays in place across page reloads and return visits in the same browser.

## Open your cart

Open your cart in either of these ways:

- Click the **Cart** icon in the header. The icon shows how many items are in your cart.
- Click **View Cart** in the notification that appears after you add an item.

The cart opens as a side drawer. If the cart is empty, it shows **Your cart is empty.**

## Cart items

Each item in the cart shows the product image, name, price, and a summary of its category and variant, such as `SHAPE, Level 2: 200mg`. Click an item to open its product detail page.

Each product and variant combination is one line in your cart. Adding more of the same variant increases that line's quantity. Different variants of the same product appear as separate lines.

## Update quantities

Use the **+** and **−** buttons on an item to change its quantity. The subtotal updates as you go.

## Remove an item

Remove an item in either of these ways:

- Click the **Remove product** button on the item.
- Click **−** when the quantity is 1.

Either way, HBS Labs asks **Remove product?** to confirm. Click **Remove** to remove the item, or **Cancel** to keep it.

## Proceed to checkout

The bottom of the cart shows the **Subtotal**. Taxes and shipping are calculated at checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Certify research use">
    Select **I certify that these products are for research use only and not for human or veterinary use.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to checkout">
    Click **Proceed to Checkout**. The button stays disabled until you select the certification checkbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

HBS Labs sends your cart's contents as the order's line items when you check out.
