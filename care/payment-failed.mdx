---
title: "Resolve a failed payment"
description: "Understand why a checkout payment failed and how to retry or switch cards."
---

If HBS Care can't charge your card when you place an order, checkout shows **Payment Failed** with the amount and the reason. Your order details are kept so you can try again without re-entering them.

## Why a payment fails

The failure screen explains what happened:

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A reason from your card issuer, such as "Card declined by issuer." | Your bank or card issuer declined the charge. | Check your card status with your bank, or use another card. |
| Your payment could not be processed. Please check your card status, or try another payment method. | The charge was declined without a specific reason. | Check your card status, or use another card. |
| We couldn't complete your payment because of a problem on our end. Please try again in a few minutes. | A temporary HBS Care or payment processor problem. Your card isn't the cause. | Wait a few minutes and select **Try Again**. |
| Our system shows you already have an active subscription for this item. | You already subscribe to this care plan. | Don't place a duplicate order. If you think this is wrong, select **Contact support**. |

## Retry the payment

On the **Payment Failed** screen:

- Select **Try Again** to resubmit the same order with the same card.
- Select **Change Payment Method** to go back to the payment step and pick or add a different card. See [Manage payment methods](/care/payment-methods).
- Select **Contact support** if the problem continues.
