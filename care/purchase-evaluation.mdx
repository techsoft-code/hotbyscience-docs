After you finish the intake questionnaire, your next step in HBS Care is the **Physician Evaluation & Labs** purchase. It covers the blood test panel and physician review needed to prepare your personalized care plan.

## What's included

The **Initial Physician Review & Care Plan** costs $165 and includes:

- **Comprehensive baseline labs**: a full biomarker panel drawn at any Quest Lab near you.
- **Analysis of 50+ biomarkers**: your test results are analyzed and your biological profile is created.
- **Physician-reviewed plan**: a board-certified physician reviews and finalizes your plan.

Most results are ready within 3-5 business days of your blood draw. HBS Care emails you as soon as your plan is ready.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to HBS Care.
- You have completed the intake questionnaire.

## Purchase the evaluation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start checkout">
    On the home screen, find the **Physician Evaluation & Labs** step and select **Purchase Physician Evaluation & Labs**. You aren't charged until you place the order on the final step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your billing address">
    Fill in your name and billing address, then select **Continue to Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method">
    Pick a saved card or add a new one. See [Manage payment methods](/care/payment-methods) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and purchase">
    Check the subtotal, sales tax, and total. Select the checkbox to accept the Terms & Conditions and consent to being charged for the assessment, then select **Purchase**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While the payment processes, keep the page open and don't press back. When the payment succeeds, HBS Care shows **Payment Successful** with the total charged. Watch your email for next steps in setting up your lab testing.

If the payment doesn't go through, see [Resolve a failed payment](/care/payment-failed).

## After you purchase

On the home screen, the **Physician Evaluation & Labs** step stays marked as in progress while your labs and clinical review are underway. Once your physician finishes the review, the **Physician Care Plan** step shows as **Ready**. Select **Review Hormone Care Plan** to see your recommendations. See [Build your hormone care plan](/care/hormone-care-plan).
