HBS Care saves your cards so you can pay for care plans and subscriptions without re-entering your details. Your default card is preselected at checkout. Card payments are processed by PayJunction.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to HBS Care.
- You have a Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, or JCB card.

## Add a payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payments">
    Go to the **Payments** page and select **Add Payment Method** (or **Add New Payment Method**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card details">
    Fill in **Cardholder Name**, **Card Number**, **Expiration Date** (MM/YY), and **CVV / CVC**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether it's your default">
    Turn on **Set as default payment method** to make this card your default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save Payment Method**. HBS Care confirms with **Payment method added successfully!**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your first payment method is automatically set as default. The toggle is locked on until you add another card.
</Note>

If a field is missing or invalid, the form shows an error under it, such as **Invalid card number**, **Card has expired**, or **Invalid CVV**.

## Change your default payment method

Your default card shows a **Default** badge in the payments list and at checkout.

1. On the **Payments** page, select the card you want to use.
2. Turn on **Set as default payment method**.
3. Select **Save Changes**. HBS Care confirms with **Payment method updated successfully!**

You can't change the card number, expiration date, or CVV of a saved card. To use different card details, add a new card.

## Delete a payment method

1. On the **Payments** page, select the card.
2. Select **Delete Payment Method**, then confirm with **Delete**.

HBS Care confirms with **Payment method deleted successfully!**

## Pay at checkout

On the payment step of checkout, HBS Care lists your **Saved Payment Methods** and preselects your default card. If you have no default, it preselects your first card. Select a different card to use it for this order.

To pay with a new card, select **Add New Payment Method** and enter the card details. HBS Care saves the card to your account. The **Set as default payment method** toggle starts off unless this is your first card, so your existing default stays in place unless you turn it on.
