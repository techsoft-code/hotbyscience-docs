Your hormone care plan lists the therapies your physician recommends after reviewing your lab results. You choose which therapies to include, then check out to start a monthly subscription that ships your medication to you.

## Prerequisites

- You have [purchased your physician evaluation and labs](/care/purchase-evaluation).
- Your physician has finished reviewing your results, and the **Physician Care Plan** step on the home screen shows **Ready**.

## Review your recommendations

On the home screen, select **Review Hormone Care Plan** (or **Review & Start** on the **Hormone Care Plan** card). The **Physician Recommendations** page lists each recommended medication with:

- Its dose and usage instructions.
- **Why was this recommended?**: your physician's reason for the recommendation.
- Its monthly price.

<Note>
  Review these recommendations with your physician before starting treatment. A prescription may be required depending on your location.
</Note>

## Add or remove therapies

- Select **Add to Plan** on a medication card to include it.
- Select **Remove from Plan** to take it out.
- Clear a medication's checkbox in the summary at the bottom of the page to remove it.

The summary shows every selected medication and the plan **Total**. It updates as you make changes.

### Choose a delivery method

Some medications come in more than one form. These cards show **Choose one delivery method** with a list of options, each with its own dose, instructions, and price. Select the option you want before or after you add the medication. If the medication is already in your plan, selecting a different option swaps it.

The chosen delivery method appears as a label next to the medication in the summary and on your order. Medications with only one form show just the medication name.

### Auto-selected medications

Some medications must be taken together. When you add one of these, HBS Care adds the companion medication too and tags it **Auto-selected with** the medication that triggered it. The summary marks it as **Required**.

You can't remove an auto-selected medication while the medication that triggered it is in your plan. Remove the triggering medication first. The companion then shows **Remove from Plan** like any other item.

## Check out

When you've selected at least one medication, select **Continue**. Checkout has four steps: **Shipping**, **Billing**, **Payment**, and **Review**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your blood pressure (if asked)">
    Some therapies require a normal blood pressure. If your plan includes one, HBS Care asks for a recent reading before shipping. See [Enter your blood pressure](#enter-your-blood-pressure).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your shipping address">
    Medication ships only to the United States and Canada. Fill in your address, then select **Continue to Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your billing address">
    Select **Same as shipping address** to reuse it, or enter a different billing address. Billing addresses can be in any country. Select **Continue to Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method">
    Pick a saved card or add a new one. See [Manage payment methods](/care/payment-methods).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and place your order">
    Check your items, sales tax, shipping, and total. Your shipping address determines the sales tax. Select the checkbox to accept the Terms & Conditions and consent to being charged monthly for the care plan, then select **Place Order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If sales tax can't be calculated, select **Retry Tax Calculation** on the review step.

If you leave checkout before placing your order, HBS Care asks you to confirm. Your progress is lost if you leave.

When the order succeeds, HBS Care shows **Order Confirmed** with the items purchased, shipping address, payment method, and total charged. Updates are sent by email. If the payment fails, see [Resolve a failed payment](/care/payment-failed).

## Enter your blood pressure

Your clinician requires a recent blood pressure reading to assess whether certain medications are appropriate for you. HBS Care shows the **Important Input** step only when your plan includes one of these medications.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Measure your blood pressure">
    Sit quietly for 5 minutes with your feet flat on the floor and your arm supported at heart level. Use a properly fitting upper-arm blood pressure cuff. Take two readings at least 1 minute apart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your reading">
    Fill in:

    - **Systolic (mmHg)**: between 70 and 250.
    - **Diastolic (mmHg)**: between 40 and 150, and lower than systolic.
    - **Date of measurement**: today or earlier.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and continue">
    Select the checkbox to confirm that the readings were measured with a blood pressure device and are accurate. Then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A reading below 140/90 mmHg takes you to the shipping step. If either number is at or above that limit, HBS Care shows **Please check your blood pressure again** and you can't continue checkout with that reading. Rest for 5 minutes, take two more measurements at least 1 minute apart, and select **Return to Care Plan** to try again. Your clinician reviews your reading to decide whether the medication is appropriate.
