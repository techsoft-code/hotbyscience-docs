description: "Complete your Hot by Science health intake questionnaire and track your initial assessment."
---

The intake questionnaire is the first step of your Hot by Science care plan. You answer questions about your health, medications, and body measurements. A physician then uses your answers, together with your lab results, to prepare your personalized care plan.

## Before you start

- You need a signed-in Hot by Science account.
- You must be at least 18 years old.
- You must be physically located in California or New York at the time of your consultation.

## Start the questionnaire

After you sign in, Hot by Science checks the status of your current care plan:

- If you haven't finished the questionnaire, Hot by Science opens it automatically.
- If you have already submitted it, Hot by Science opens your home screen with your care plan progress.

You can only open the questionnaire while your care plan is pending. After you submit it, your answers are locked and you can't retake the assessment.

## Answer the questions

The questionnaire is split into steps. Each step groups related questions by category. The progress bar at the top shows **Step X of Y**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer each question on the step">
    Questions can be single choice, multiple choice, dates, numbers, or free text. Required questions show **Please answer this question to continue** if you leave them blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next Step">
    Hot by Science saves all of your answers for that step when you click **Next Step**. If the save fails, you see **Failed to save your answers. Please try again.** Click **Next Step** again to retry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Save Changes on the last step">
    The last step shows **Save Changes** instead of **Next Step**. Clicking it submits your questionnaire and returns you to the home screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your answers are only saved when you click **Next Step** or **Save Changes**. If you leave a step before clicking either button, the changes on that step are not saved.
</Note>

To revisit an earlier step, click its segment in the progress bar. You can only jump to steps you have already reached.

### Follow-up questions

Some answers unlock a follow-up question. The follow-up appears directly under the answer that unlocks it. For example, selecting a current medication asks you for details about that medication.

If you deselect the answer that unlocked a follow-up, Hot by Science clears the follow-up's answer.

### Multiple-choice questions with "None"

In multiple-choice questions, **None** is exclusive. Selecting **None** clears your other selections. Selecting another option clears **None**.

### Sex-specific questions

The questionnaire shows different questions depending on your sex. For example:

- **Female questions** include whether you have a uterus, your menstrual pattern, the date of your last natural menstrual period, and details about estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, or thyroid medications.
- **Male questions** include details about testosterone, thyroid, or clomiphene, enclomiphene, or hCG medications.

Both sets of questions ask about medication allergies. If you select **Other**, you describe the allergy.

### Measurements

Weight, height, and waist circumference questions on a step share one unit selector. Choose **Imperial (lbs, ft/in)** or **Metric (kg, cm)**. Your browser remembers this choice after a page refresh.

### Eligibility checks

Some answers stop you from continuing:

| Check | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Your date of birth makes you younger than 18 | **You must be at least 18 years old to continue.** |
| You're located outside California or New York | **Care through Hot by Science requires you to be physically located in California or New York at the time of your consultation. Please check back if your location changes.** |

## View your initial assessment

After you submit the questionnaire, your home screen shows your care plan progress in three stages:

1. **Intake Questionnaire**: Marked complete, with the date you finished it.
2. **Physician Evaluation & Labs**: Your current stage while labs and physician review are in progress.
3. **Physician Care Plan**: Locked until your physician evaluation is complete.

### Purchase the physician evaluation

Before you purchase, the **Physician Evaluation & Labs** card shows the **Initial Physician Review & Care Plan** and its price. The purchase covers the required blood test panel and the physician review needed to prepare your care plan. It includes:

- **Comprehensive baseline labs**: Full biomarker panel drawn at any Quest Lab near you.
- **Analysis of 50+ biomarkers**: Your test results are analyzed and your biological profile is created.
- **Physician-reviewed plan**: A board-certified physician reviews and finalizes your plan.

Click **Purchase Physician Evaluation & Labs** to buy it.

After you purchase, the card collapses. Click it to expand the details. Most results are ready within 3-5 business days of your blood draw. Hot by Science emails you as soon as your plan is ready.

### Review your care plan

When your physician evaluation is complete, **Physician Evaluation & Labs** is marked done and the **Physician Care Plan** card unlocks so you can review your plan.
