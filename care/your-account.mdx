Your Care account gives you access to your health assessment, care plan, and payments. This page walks you through creating an account, verifying your email, signing in, and resetting your password.

<Note>
  Buttons such as **Continue**, **Confirm**, and **Sign In** stay disabled until every required field is filled in correctly. Hover over a disabled button to see what's missing.
</Note>

## Create an account

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- An email address you can access right away
- A phone number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    On the **Sign up to unlock full experience** screen, enter your email and click **Continue**.

    If you see **This email is already in use.**, you already have an account. [Sign in](#sign-in) instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Hot by Science sends a verification email to your address. Verify your email in one of two ways:

    - **Enter the code.** On the **Check your email for OTP** screen, type the 6-digit code from the email.
    - **Click the link.** Click **Verify My Email** in the email. See [Verify your email from the link](#verify-your-email-from-the-link).

    If the email doesn't arrive, wait for the timer to finish and click **Resend code**. You can request a new code every 60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your details">
    On the **Create your account** screen, fill in:

    - **First name** and **Last name**
    - **Phone number.** Select your country from the picker, then enter your number. The country defaults to the United States. If you paste a number that starts with a country code, such as `+44`, Care selects the matching country for you.
    - **Password.** Your password must have 8-20 characters, 1 uppercase and 1 lowercase letter, 1 number (0-9), and 1 special character (`!@#$%^&*`). A checklist below the field shows which requirements you've met.
    - **Sex.** Select **Female** or **Male**.

    Check the box to accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice, then click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get started">
    Care shows **Account created successfully** and signs you in. After 3 seconds, Care takes you into the app. Click **Get Started** to go right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you refresh the page or use your browser's back button during sign-up, Care returns you to the step you were on.
</Tip>

### Verify your email from the link

Instead of typing the code, you can click **Verify My Email** in the verification email.

1. Click **Verify My Email** in the email. A **Verify your email** page opens.
2. Click **Verify My Email**.
3. Care confirms your email and takes you to the **Create your account** screen. Continue from [Add your details](#create-an-account).

If you still have the sign-up tab open on the code screen in the same browser, that tab also moves to **Create your account**. You can finish in either tab.

If the link doesn't work, Care shows one of these screens:

| Screen | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You're signed in to another account** | You're signed in to Care with a different account in this browser. | Click **Sign out and continue** to verify the new email, or **Stay signed in** to keep your current session. |
| **This link has expired** | Verification links are only valid for a limited time. | Click **Sign up again** and enter your email to get a new link. |
| **This link is no longer valid** | A newer verification email was sent, or the account is already set up. | Use the link from your most recent email, or click **Sign In** if you've already created your account. |
| **We couldn't verify your email** | Something went wrong on our end. | Click **Try again**. |

### Sign-up messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **The verification code is incorrect. Please try again.** | Check the code in your most recent email and enter it again. |
| **This email is already verified. Please sign in to continue.** | Care takes you to the sign-in screen. Sign in with your email and password. |
| **Your signup session has expired. Please verify your email again.** | Care returns you to the email step. Enter your email to get a new code. |

## Sign in

1. On the **Welcome back** screen, enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign In**.

Don't have an account yet? Click **Sign Up** at the bottom of the screen.

## Reset your password

If you forget your password, reset it with a code sent to your email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a reset code">
    On the sign-in screen, click **Forgot password?**. On the **Reset your password** screen, enter your email and click **Continue**.

    To leave without resetting, click **Back**. Care returns you to the page you came from, or to the sign-in screen if you opened the reset page directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    On the **Check your email for OTP** screen, type the 6-digit code from the email.

    If the email doesn't arrive, wait for the timer to finish and click **Resend code**. You can request a new code every 60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    On the **Enter new password** screen, enter your **New password** and type it again in **Confirm new password**. The new password must meet the same requirements as when you [created your account](#create-an-account). Click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Care shows **Password changed!**. After 3 seconds, Care takes you to the sign-in screen. Click **Sign In** to go right away, then sign in with your new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Password reset messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **No account found with this email address.** | Check the email for typos, or [create an account](#create-an-account). |
| **This account hasn't finished signup yet.** | Click **Finish signing up** to complete the sign-up steps for that email. |
| **The verification code has expired. Please request a new one.** | Care returns you to the email step. Enter your email again to get a new code. |
| **Passwords do not match.** | Make sure both password fields are the same. |
