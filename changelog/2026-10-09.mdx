These are the Hot by Science Care, CMS, and Labs updates shipped in the week ending Friday, October 9, 2026. Each day's changes are listed separately; use the right sidebar to jump to a date.

<Update label="October 9, 2026" tags={["CMS"]}>
  **New**

  - **CMS**: Clinical lab details now include a [table of contents](/cms/clinical-lab-results#navigate-with-the-table-of-contents) for faster navigation.
  - **CMS**: Download the original lab report from a clinical lab.
</Update>

<Update label="October 8, 2026" tags={["Care","CMS"]}>
  **New**

  - **CMS**: View the patient list.
  - **CMS**: Filter labs by status, preview a requisition, and mark it as sent.
  - **CMS**: Enter and edit lab results, then mark a lab as completed. Lab actions are available directly from the clinical lab detail page. [Learn more](/cms/clinical-lab-results).

  **Improved**

  - **Care**: Forgot password now has a back button, and sign-in screens have a refreshed background.
  - **Care**: Saved payment methods show card brand icons, including American Express and JCB, and clearly mark your default card.
</Update>

<Update label="October 7, 2026" tags={["Labs"]}>
  **Improved**

  - **Labs**: Product pages have a new quantity selector, and your cart shows the selected variant level for each item.
  - **Labs**: Updated the quality section on the home page for compliance.

  **Fixed**

  - **Labs**: Terms and Privacy Policy pages no longer show the research-use confirmation, and their text formatting is now consistent.
</Update>

<Update label="October 6, 2026" tags={["Care","Labs"]}>
  **New**

  - **Care**: Add your phone number when you sign up.
  - **Labs**: Add your phone number at checkout.

  **Improved**

  - **Care**: The intake questionnaire shows waist measurement guidance tailored to your sex, with a cleaner question layout.

  **Fixed**

  - **Labs**: The product image gallery now stays in view as you scroll the product page.
</Update>
