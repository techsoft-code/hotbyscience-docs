These are the Hot by Science Care, CMS, and Labs updates shipped in the week ending Friday, October 16, 2026. Each day's changes are listed separately; use the right sidebar to jump to a date.

<Update label="October 16, 2026" tags={["CMS"]}>
  **New**

  - **CMS**: The **Peptide Research** section of the [clinical lab detail view](/cms/clinical-lab-results#navigate-with-the-table-of-contents) now shows the patient's generated peptide pathways, including product, amount, level, units per injection, and frequency.

  **Improved**

  - **CMS**: Patient lists on the [Clinical & Lab page](/cms/lab-requisitions#filter-patients-by-lab-status) are now sorted by most recently updated care plan. The **Updated Date** column shows when the care plan last changed.
</Update>

<Update label="October 13, 2026" tags={["Care","CMS","Labs"]}>
  **New**

  - **Care**: Verify your email by clicking the link in your sign-up email. If a link has expired or been replaced, you see a clear message explaining what to do next.
  - **Care**: Before checking out hormone therapies, you now enter your blood pressure. Guidance on how to measure it is shown, and you're asked to check again if a reading looks out of range.
  - **CMS**: Admins can reset their password.
  - **CMS**: Download lab requisition details.
  - **Care**, **Labs**: A friendly "page not found" screen now appears when you open a link that doesn't exist.

  **Improved**

  - **Care**: On mobile, the forgot password buttons stay pinned to the bottom of the screen.

  **Under the hood**

  - **Care**, **CMS**, **Labs**: Added a staging environment for testing releases before they go live.
</Update>
